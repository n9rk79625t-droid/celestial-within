Moon Practice Ideas

New: write one intention and choose one grounded action.

Waxing: build, practice, nourish momentum.

Full: illuminate what has become visible; gratitude and release reflection.

Waning: simplify, integrate, rest, clear your space.

Practice Ideas

Place the card where you can see it while journaling. Write one quality from the card you want to embody. Create a small symbolic drawing inspired by its imagery. At day’s end, note where its theme appeared.

Meditation Ideas

Visual journey into the card • three-minute symbol focus • breathe while observing one color or object • imagine asking the card’s figure one reflective question.
